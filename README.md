## Bondi 9 vs Bondi 8: what actually changed

The Bondi 9 is lighter and slightly taller. Compared with the Bondi 8, the men's version dropped from 10.6 oz to 9.3 oz, the heel stack went from 43 mm to 44 mm, and the drop moved from 4 mm to 5 mm. Price went the other direction: $175 against the Bondi 8's $165.

What that feels like in practice is a shoe that carries its cushioning with less mass underfoot than the previous version. The Bondi 8's selling point was always the sheer amount of foam — 43 mm of stack is a lot for a daily trainer — and reviewers consistently described the ride as smooth rather than sporty. The 9 keeps that character with roughly 1.3 oz less material to move.

If you already own a Bondi 8 and it's not worn out, the upgrade case is thin. You'd be paying $10 more for a lighter shoe with a slightly different geometry, and geometry changes are exactly the kind of thing that either suits your stride or doesn't. If you're choosing between the two for a first pair and both are in stock, the older model is frequently the better value — Bondi 8 stock clears at a real discount while the 9 holds closer to list.

## Who the Bondi is genuinely built for

The Bondi is a neutral, max-cushion daily trainer. EatingWell's roundup of podiatrist recommendations describes the Bondi 9 as one of the most commonly recommended shoes for lower back pain, joint pain, high arches and plantar fasciitis, crediting the plush midsole, arch support and extended heel. That description matches how Hoka positions the shoe: the "plush" end of its range, with heel-to-toe drop listed alongside everyday running as the primary use case.

Where it gets overstated: "max cushion" is not the same as "orthopaedic" and it isn't a fix for anything. It's a soft, stable-feeling shoe that a lot of people find comfortable standing and walking in all day. It's also heavy by the standards of the rest of the market, and nobody is racing in a Bondi. If you want one shoe for both a 10-mile easy run and a day on your feet, this category exists precisely for that.
